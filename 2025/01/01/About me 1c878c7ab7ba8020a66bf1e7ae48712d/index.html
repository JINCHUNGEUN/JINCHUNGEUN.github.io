About Me

Posted on 2025-01-01 Edited on 2025-04-01

👋 Hi, I’m CHUNGEN JIN !

I am a full-stack engineer with a strong focus on backend development, primarily using Python and Golang. My expertise includes building scalable and efficient systems with Django and Flask, while also working on Vue.js for frontend solutions.

💼 Professional Experience

Over the years, I have worked on various cutting-edge projects, including:

🔹 Robot Operations & Maintenance System Development

  • Led the development of a robot operations and maintenance system, focusing on intelligent diagnostics and automated robot software package upgrades.

🔹 Entrepreneurial Project: “数说前程” WeChat Mini-Program

  • Designed and developed a WeChat Mini-Program aimed at helping students efficiently navigate their Gaokao (college entrance exam) application process.
  • Implemented an intelligent recommendation system to suggest optimal universities and majors based on student preferences and scores.
  • Built a scalable backend infrastructure to support high user traffic during peak admission periods.

🔹 Entrepreneurial Project: Overseas Travel Map & E-commerce Platform

  • Developed a mobile travel map app for overseas travelers, providing detailed localized navigation and real-time travel insights.

  • Focused on user experience and seamless multi-language support to enhance usability for international tourists.
